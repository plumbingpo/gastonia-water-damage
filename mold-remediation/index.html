Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. Gastonia Restoration sends IICRC-certified technicians to homes and commercial buildings across Gastonia, where they contain mold, remove it and keep it from returning under strict industry protocols.

Wiping away the mold you can see is not remediation. Done professionally, the work includes finding where the moisture comes from, containing the area so spores cannot cross-contaminate other rooms, taking out affected material under HEPA filtration, applying antimicrobials to what remains and correcting the moisture issue to prevent a return. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

First comes a full assessment of the property, frequently with air quality tests that identify the kinds of spores in the air and how concentrated they are. With that data we can build a remediation plan aimed at exactly what your property needs. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • A vapor barrier is worth considering in a crawl space, which also needs correct ventilation
  • Laundry rooms, baths and kitchens all need working exhaust ventilation that is sized for the space
  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
  • Aim for 30 to 50% indoor humidity and never let it stay above 60%, running dehumidifiers and ventilating as needed
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment
